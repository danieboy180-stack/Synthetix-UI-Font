v2 also matches Pinterest’s proportions: 24 letter widths, ascender, descender and dot heights are tuned from the pangram at three display sizes. Stems sit within 1.3% of the measured weights; plain Instrument Sans only offers 400 and 700, so its bold is about 14% too heavy.

How to use it

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/synthetix-ui-font.css">

<p class="sx-body">Body text</p>
<p class="sx-label">Row label</p>
<h1 class="sx-h1">Headline</h1>

Or in your own CSS: font-family:"Synthetix UI"; font-size:16px; The fonts carry size-adjust:108%, so 16px renders the measured body size. No letter-spacing needed; tracking is baked in.

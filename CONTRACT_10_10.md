# The 10/10 contract (Stage 0)

**Provenance.** Thresholds are copied unchanged from *Synthetix UI V4: Master Plan to 10/10*, sections 3 and 6. This file was written during the V4.0.10 run, not before it (the plan asked for a pre-work commit).
The only thresholds added by the run are the subset byte budgets, written to `build/budgets.json` *before* the first export, and the confusables threshold (Jaccard 0.65), written into `tools/confusables.py` before its first run. No threshold was changed after seeing results.

**10/10 = all hard gates pass, both scorecards at least 95/100, identity review signed off, zero open critical defects, nothing waived.** Fidelity (match to reference, verifiable measures only) and Quality are never blended.

## Hard gates (any failure blocks release)
Font Bakery universal + Google Fonts profiles 0 FAIL on every shipped file; Latin Core + Pri-African coverage and language-shaping checks; tabular figures equal width and no .notdef for required characters;
clean-clone build reproduces recorded SHA-256; variable and static agree within 1 unit; advance widths identical across Chromium, Firefox, WebKit and native apps; OFL text/copyright intact and name clearance recorded; zero open critical defects.

## Fidelity scorecard (95 or more; pre-registered)
Shape similarity of at least 0.85 on 90 percent of Match glyphs; widths within 1 px for 90 percent; held-out pair gaps within 1 px for 90 percent; vertical metrics all within 1 px; stems within 5 percent in all four weights, measured from real references.

## Quality scorecard (95 or more)
Font Bakery pass rate; coverage; small-size legibility and confusables; human review of named kerning pairs; cross-engine rendering; token adoption on real screens.

## Identity review (pass/fail)
Two reviewers, using `IDENTITY_DIRECTION.md`, tell Synthetix, Instrument Sans and the reference apart in a blind comparison and say why.

# Review of the master directive — what blocks a real 10/10, and amendments (additions only; no threshold weakened)

1. **Goal conflict.** "Perfect Pinterest Sans" vs "independent identity". Pinterest Sans is proprietary (Grilli Type); outline similarity as a target = copying. Amendment: allow *functional-parity* metrics only (x-height ratio, aperture/legibility at 12–16 px, vertical-cut terminals for tight spacing); forbid outline-similarity scores and reference-outline overlays.
2. **Measurement-table labels** (see PROVENANCE): n/O "/ cap" are /x-height. Amendment: re-derive every ratio from raw pixels in the JSON, store with units.
3. **Self-scoring is circular.** The 95/100 cards are filled by the author. Amendment: pre-register measurable proxies now (below), and make only independent reviewers' sheets count for design categories. I have not scored them.
4. **Hard gates 10–12 (legal, 2 reviewers, owner approval) cannot be closed by AI** — mark EXTERNAL with owner and due date; they stay FAILED until a human records them.
5. **Skeleton + thicker stroke does not make a weight family.** Verified: construction collapses at stem ≥112 (s,6,9,apertures). Amendment: per-master geometry parameters (aperture angles, terminal widths, counter minimums) and automatic *weight-validity gates*: same contour topology as Regular, min counter area ≥ X, aperture width ≥ Y at 12 px, fit error ≤ 1.5u.
6. **Interpolation gate needs numbers and intermediate masters.** Register: intermediate instances (350/450/550/650) vs direct builds: area ≤ 2%, bbox ≤ 2u. Measured now: 350 → Y 6.5% area, six 3u (FAIL); 450 → 1.05%, 1.5u (PASS).
7. **Tooling blockers.** The offline sandbox lacks fontmake, ufoLib2, HarfBuzz, FreeType bindings (only Pillow-FreeType/Raqm), Fontspector/FontBakery, skia-pathops, brotli. Amendment: run the release gates in CI with pinned requirements; keep the sandbox for drawing.
8. **African-language scope needs a per-language matrix.** Hausa (ɓ ɗ ƙ ƴ, ʼ; check Niger/Nigeria differences), Yoruba (ẹ ọ ṣ + stacked tone marks ẹ́ ọ̀), Igbo (ị ọ ụ, ń ǹ ḿ, dot-below + tone stacking), plus Nigerian Pidgin and other Latin-script languages in Stackra's markets. Amendment: native-reader review gate per language; GPOS mark-to-base/mark-to-mark stacking tests with real words.
9. **Confusables thresholds are unset.** Register (14 px Jaccard, lower = better): I/l ≤ 0.60, l/1 ≤ 0.30, O/0 ≤ 0.45, rn/m ≤ 0.40, cl/d ≤ 0.45. Measured Regular: I/l 0.67 (**FAIL**), l/1 0.12, O/0 0.40, rn/m 0.32, cl/d 0.43.
10. **Add:** vertical-metrics clipping tests per OS; hinting/ttfautohint decision; license/ownership chain (commissioning, employee contributions); name clearance; fallback/size budget; accessibility (dyslexia-sensitive pairs); automated regression snapshots of every glyph.

# Identity system — decision record (evidence class in brackets)

**Chosen direction: A "Quiet Precision"** [PROPOSED]. Vertical-stress round forms, k=0.575 super-elliptic curves, vertically cut terminals, double-storey a, single-storey g, tailed l, large x-height (515/700 = 0.736).

## Honest account of the A/B/C exercise
A, B and C were produced by one drawing engine with different parameters (curve tension, stress, terminal cut, shoulder angle). At 12–48 px they are barely distinguishable. They are **parameter variants, not three independent concepts**, so this is weak evidence for a choice. Measured confusable-pair similarity (Jaccard, 14 px; lower = more distinct): mean A 0.430, B 0.426, C 0.412 — differences < 0.02 [VERIFIED]. No measured criterion separates them; A was chosen on proportional fit to the historical observations (tall x-height, near-circular o, vertical terminals) and engine maturity. A real concept stage needs hand-drawn alternatives and a human art-director decision.

## Design grammar (frozen only for Regular-range work; subject to human review)
Terminals vertical cut; overshoot 12; stress h/v = 0.90; round letters share one ring primitive (o c e d b p q a g use the same outer/inner ellipse logic with per-glyph clipping); diagonals specified by horizontal thickness (0.97·stem); l tail added after measurement showed I≈l (Jaccard 1.00 → 0.67).
